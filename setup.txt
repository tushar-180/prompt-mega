SETUP WORKFLOW, BDE DIGITAL PROSPECTING SYSTEM

I am setting up a pre-sales prospecting workflow for our software development and IT services business.

The purpose of this run is to build the exact workspace structure, folders, lists, sheets, tabs, fields, and rules that the later "Digital Prospect Audit & Outreach" master workflow depends on. Everything the master workflow reads or writes has to exist and be named exactly as written here, otherwise that run will fail partway through.

This is a SETUP RUN ONLY.

Do not research prospects.
Do not create outreach.
Do not send emails.
Do not invent existing company data.

All dates in this system use the Asia/Kolkata time zone. Any date field you create, any date you record, and any date rule you write is read in that time zone (time zone: Asia/Kolkata).

OPPORTUNITY SCORING TAB

Fit With Approved Service
