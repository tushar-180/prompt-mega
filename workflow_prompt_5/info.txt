Situation

A BDE named Rahul has a product demo with ABC Technologies tomorrow at 3 PM.

ABC Technologies is currently on the Pro Plan.

Rahul prepares some notes for the call:

“We will show the customer our AI Assistant and Advanced Analytics. Both features are available on their plan.”

Now imagine there is no workflow.

Before using the workflow
Step 1: BDE checks the upcoming call

Rahul opens Salesforce and sees:

ABC Technologies
Demo Call: Tomorrow, 3 PM
Plan: Pro
Assigned BDE: Rahul

So far, everything is fine.

Step 2: BDE prepares for the call

Rahul opens his preparation notes in Notion.

He writes:

AI Assistant is available.
Advanced Analytics is available.

Maybe he remembers this information from a previous demo or from an older document.

Step 3: Rahul manually checks the product

Now Rahul has to remember:

“Wait, did the product team change anything recently?”

He may open the product changelog and search manually.

Suppose the latest changelog says:

AI Assistant has been renamed to AI Copilot.

And another update says:

Advanced Analytics is now available only on Enterprise plans.

Rahul has to notice both changes himself.

Step 4: Possible problem

Maybe Rahul doesn't check the changelog.

Then he goes into the client call and says:

“Our AI Assistant can do X.”

But the actual product name is now AI Copilot.

Even worse, he says:

“Advanced Analytics is included in your Pro Plan.”

But ABC Technologies is actually on Pro, while Advanced Analytics is Enterprise only.

Now the customer might ask:

“Really? Can you show us Advanced Analytics?”

Rahul opens the product and realizes:

“Oh... this isn't available on their plan.”

That's a bad sales experience.

The customer may think:

“Does this sales person actually know their own product?”

After using the workflow

Now let's run exactly the same situation with the workflow.

Step 1: Workflow checks Salesforce

The workflow automatically looks at Salesforce.

It finds:

Client: ABC Technologies
Call: Tomorrow, 3 PM
BDE: Rahul
Plan: Pro

So the workflow now knows:

Who is having the call + when + which BDE + customer's plan.

Step 2: Workflow opens the preparation notes

It goes to Notion and reads Rahul's preparation notes.

It finds:

“AI Assistant is available.”
“Advanced Analytics is available.”

Instead of trusting these statements, the workflow says:

“I need to verify these claims.”

Step 3: Workflow checks the latest product information

It checks the latest Notion product changelog and feature availability information.

It discovers:

Claim 1:

AI Assistant

Latest product information:

Renamed to AI Copilot.

So:

AI Assistant → RENAMED

Claim 2:

Advanced Analytics is available on the customer's plan.

Latest information:

Advanced Analytics → Enterprise only.

Salesforce says ABC Technologies has:

Pro Plan

So:

Advanced Analytics → PLAN RESTRICTED

Step 4: Workflow checks the date

This is another important part.

Suppose the changelog says:

Advanced Analytics will become Enterprise-only from September 15.

And Rahul's call is:

September 12

The workflow understands:

The feature is still available for the customer on the date of the call.

So it doesn't incorrectly flag the claim.

But if the call is:

September 20

then it flags the claim because the restriction is already effective.

So the workflow doesn't just look at what changed.

It checks:

What is true on the actual date of the customer call?

Step 5: Workflow fixes Rahul's notes

Before workflow:

“We will show AI Assistant and Advanced Analytics. Both features are available on their plan.”

After workflow:

“We will demonstrate AI Copilot. Advanced Analytics is available only on Enterprise and should not be presented as included in the customer's Pro Plan.”

Now Rahul has accurate information before entering the call.

Step 6: Workflow sends Slack notification

Because this is a material correction, the workflow sends Rahul a Slack message.

Something like:

Call Preparation Correction

ABC Technologies' demo is tomorrow at 3 PM.

AI Assistant has been renamed to AI Copilot.

Advanced Analytics is restricted to Enterprise and is not included in the customer's Pro Plan.

Please use the corrected wording during the demo.

Rahul sees this before the call.

Step 7: No repeated notifications

Imagine the workflow runs again two hours later.

Without proper logic, Rahul could receive:

Correction! Advanced Analytics is Enterprise only.

Then another:

Correction! Advanced Analytics is Enterprise only.

Then another:

Correction! Advanced Analytics is Enterprise only.

That's annoying.

The workflow keeps track of the call + changelog version + notification.

So it knows:

“I already notified Rahul about this correction for this call and this version of the product information.”

Therefore:

No duplicate Slack message.

Now compare both situations

BDE manually checks Salesforce	Workflow checks Salesforce
BDE manually finds upcoming calls	Workflow finds upcoming calls
BDE writes preparation notes	Preparation notes are automatically reviewed
BDE may forget to check changelog	Workflow checks latest changelog
BDE may use old feature name	Workflow detects renamed feature
BDE may promise unavailable feature	Workflow detects plan restriction

The real benefit

The workflow isn't really about just reading Notion.

Its real purpose is to prevent this situation:

BDE prepares → uses outdated information → promises something incorrectly → customer discovers it during demo → BDE looks unprepared.

Instead, it changes the process to:

Upcoming call found → customer plan checked → preparation checked → product information verified → outdated claims corrected → BDE notified → BDE enters call with accurate information.

Very simple way to remember it

Salesforce tells us:
👉 Who is the customer and what plan do they have?

Notion tells us:
👉 What does the BDE plan to say, and what is actually true about the product?

Workflow compares the two:
👉 Is the BDE's information correct for this customer and this call date?

Slack tells the BDE:
👉 Hey, something in your preparation needs to be corrected.

So basically, this workflow acts like a pre-call fact checker for the BDE.
