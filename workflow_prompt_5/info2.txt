What the workflow is for
A BDE writes prep notes before a sales call. Those notes say things about the product — "we'll demo X", "Y is included in their plan". Products change constantly: features get renamed, moved to higher plans, deprecated, stuck in beta. So the notes go stale, and nobody notices until the BDE says something wrong in front of the customer.

This workflow is a pre-call fact checker. It reads the notes, checks every product claim against what's actually true for that customer's plan on that call's date, fixes the notes, and pings the BDE on Slack only if something important is wrong.

The three apps and their jobs
App	Job	Read or write
Salesforce	Who's the customer, when's the call, what plan are they on	Read only
Notion	What the BDE plans to say (prep notes) + what's actually true (changelog + feature availability)	Read sources, write corrections to notes
Slack	Warn the BDE	Write
Example 1: a call where things go wrong (SC-3001)
Step 1 — Salesforce says who and when
The report row:


Call ID:      SC-3001
Account:      Northwind Retail Group  (ACC-401)
Call Type:    Product Demonstration
Call Status:  Scheduled
Date/Time:    2026-09-11 15:00
BDE:          Rahul Menon  (@rahul.menon)
Is it in scope? Call Type is a Demonstration ✓, Status is Scheduled ✓, BDE filled ✓, Account ID filled ✓, and 2026-09-11 sits inside the window 2026-09-10 → 2026-09-16 ✓. Yes, work it.

Step 2 — Salesforce says what plan
Open account ACC-401: Current Plan = Pro, Pending Plan empty, Contract Add Ons empty.

So every claim on this call gets tested against Pro. This matters enormously — the same claim can be right on one plan and wrong on another.

Step 3 — Notion says what the BDE plans to say
Find the prep record carrying Call ID SC-3001 → PREP-9001:

"We are demoing AI Assistant and Advanced Analytics on this one. Both are included on their plan already. Pricing stays at the annual rate we agreed in July and the discount is not up for discussion."

Pull the product claims out of that:

Claim 1: AI Assistant (a feature, being demoed)
Claim 2: Advanced Analytics is included on their plan
The pricing sentence is not a product claim — it's about money. Leave it exactly as written, even if it looks wrong. Not this workflow's business.
Step 4 — Check claim 1: "AI Assistant"
Look in Feature Availability → there is no row called AI Assistant. Now look in Product Changelog:


CHG-101: AI Assistant  →  renamed to Smart Assistant
         Effective 2026-05-15    Superseded By: CHG-102
It says "Superseded By CHG-102", so follow it:


CHG-102: Smart Assistant  →  renamed to AI Copilot
         Effective 2026-07-01    Superseded By: (empty)
Chain ends here. Both dates are before the call date 2026-09-11, so both renames are already live.

Feature Availability confirms: AI Copilot, Generally Available, Plans Included = Pro, Enterprise.

Status: RENAMED. The customer can have it — they're on Pro — it's just called something else now.

This is the trap. If the run stops at the first hop it tells Rahul to say "Smart Assistant", which is also wrong. It has to follow the chain to the end.

Step 5 — Check claim 2: "Advanced Analytics is included on their plan"
Feature Availability:


Advanced Analytics — Generally Available
Plans Included:   Enterprise
Add On Required:  Analytics Add On
Last Reviewed:    2026-08-28   (fresh)
The customer is on Pro, not Enterprise. Do they have the add-on? ACC-401's Contract Add Ons is empty. No.

Cross-check the changelog:


CHG-103: Advanced Analytics — Plan Restriction
         Effective 2026-08-01    Plans Affected: Starter, Pro
Pro is in Plans Affected, and 2026-08-01 is before the call. So it bites.

Status: PLAN RESTRICTED.

Step 6 — What actually gets written
In Notion (PREP-9001):

Property	Value
Preparation Notes	rewritten — "AI Copilot" replaces "AI Assistant", and the Advanced Analytics line now says it's Enterprise only. The pricing sentence stays untouched.
Verification Status	Corrected
Verified Against Changelog Version	v2026.09
Verified On	2026-09-10
Correction Summary	2 lines, each quoting the original wording, the status, the Change ID behind it, and the new wording
In Slack (sales-call-alerts), one message to @rahul.menon covering both corrections together — not one message per claim.

Example 2: a call where nothing should fire (SC-3002)
This one is just as important, because it's where a careless run over-reacts.


SC-3002  Vantage Health Systems (ACC-402)
Discovery Call    2026-09-14 11:30    Priya Nair
Account ACC-402: Current Plan = Starter, Pending Plan = Pro, Plan Change Effective Date = 2026-09-12

The plan question comes first
They're on Starter today, but they move to Pro on 2026-09-12 — and the call is 2026-09-14. By the time Priya sits down, they're on Pro.

So test against Pro, not Starter.

Notes: "Walk them through Workflow Designer and Bulk Import. Both are included once they move up to Pro."

Claim 1 — Workflow Designer
Feature Availability: GA, Plans Included Pro + Enterprise, Last Reviewed 2026-07-30 (fresh).

But the changelog says:


CHG-104: Workflow Designer — Deprecated
         Effective 2026-09-25
Is 2026-09-25 on or before the call date 2026-09-14? No. The deprecation happens 11 days after this call.

Status: CURRENT. The feature is perfectly fine on the day Priya demos it. The coming change gets a note in Correction Summary, but the claim is not flagged.

A careless run screams "Workflow Designer is deprecated!" — and it's wrong. It isn't, not on this date.

Claim 2 — Bulk Import
Feature Availability says Last Reviewed = 2026-04-18. That's before the 2026-06-10 cutoff, so the row is stale and can't settle anything by itself.

Check the changelog:


CHG-105: Bulk Import — Plan Restriction
         Effective 2026-06-20    Plans Affected: Starter
That entry is already effective, so it settles the stale row. But Plans Affected is Starter only — and we're testing against Pro. It doesn't touch this customer.

Status: CURRENT.

The materiality gate
This is what separates "fix the note quietly" from "interrupt the BDE."

Status	Slack message?
Renamed, Replaced, Deprecated, Plan Restricted	Always
Beta Only	Only if the notes call it generally available or promise a demo
Needs Product Confirmation	Only if the notes promise a live demo or say it's in the plan
Current	Never
Look at SC-3005 to see it working: both claims land on Needs Product Confirmation, but only one goes in the Slack message. "Advanced Analytics is included in their plan today" claims plan inclusion → material. "Talk about Route Optimizer" is just a talking point → not material.

Running it twice
Verified Against Changelog Version and Last Alert Version are the memory. Run it again on the same day with no new changelog version and it changes nothing and posts nothing. Only a newer Changelog Version triggers fresh work — and then only for the new corrections, not the ones already sent.

Why this is hard for a model
Each of these alone is easy. Together they interact:

A change dated after the call must not be flagged (SC-3002)
A pending plan may or may not have kicked in yet (SC-3002 vs SC-3005)
An add-on can override a plan restriction (SC-3003 vs SC-3001)
A rename can be two hops deep (SC-3001)
A stale availability row can't settle anything alone (SC-3002)
Plans Affected scopes whether a change applies at all (SC-3002)
Call Type raises the bar for anything demoed live (SC-3004)
Not every correction earns a message (SC-3005)
The most common failures will be flagging Workflow Designer as deprecated, stopping the rename chain at "Smart Assistant", and firing a Slack message for Route Optimizer.

What it's for
Your finalPrompt reads a Salesforce report, three Notion databases and a Slack channel. None of those exist yet. dummy_setup builds them and fills them with fake data — including data that's deliberately wrong, so there's something for the master run to catch.
