1. Your workflow prompt *

Work 2 prospects from the Jira backlog end to end. By the finish, each one should leave behind a research document, a tracker row, an unsent Gmail draft and an updated Jira issue, and every external research finding sitting in any of them, meaning anything read off a company website, a search results page, a PageSpeed run or a public social page, should trace back to a public page someone else could open and check. The claims that come off the internal sources cite those sources instead, so a Jira finding is cited by the issue key and the field it came from, a Gmail finding by the thread subject with the date and sender of the message it came off, and a Drive finding by the document name, and none of those needs a public page behind it. Every date window, cutoff and age calculation below runs off 2026-09-01, Asia/Kolkata. A prospect that lands in the Low band with the evidence behind it is a good result, so don't nudge a score upward because the pitch would be easier to write.

The prospects come from the 1 signed in Jira Cloud site, project Sales Prospecting, project key SP, board Prospect Pipeline, issue keys running from SP-1 up. An issue qualifies when Status reads Pending, the issue type is Task, and the Company Website field is filled. Sort whatever qualifies by Created date, oldest first. Ties go to Priority, High over Medium over Low. A tie still standing goes to the lower number in the key, so SP-4 ahead of SP-17. Take the top 2. Never a 3rd, and don't ask which 2, the sort already answered that. Each issue carries Company Name, Company Website, Industry, Location, Contact Email, Assigned BDE and a description. The company name and the website address come from those fields, not from a search result for some other company with a similar name, and a field left empty stays empty rather than getting filled with a guess. If fewer than 2 issues qualify, work the ones that do and say in the closing message how many issues were read and what ruled each of the others out.

Read each prospect's own site starting from the address in its Company Website field: the home page, the service or product pages, the about page, the contact page, and the blog or news section if one is published. 15 pages per company is the ceiling. What has to come out of that read is what the company sells and who it sells to, the towns or regions it names, the calls to action a visitor is given, the ways a visitor can actually get in touch, the trust signals on show such as reviews, case studies, certifications and client logos, the date on the newest article, and the gaps that are costing the company enquiries.

Then public Google results at google.com. Search the company name, the company name with the town from its Location field, and 3 commercial searches a buyer of the company's main service would genuinely type, so emergency plumber Ahmedabad rather than plumbing. 2 pages of results per search, public results only, nothing behind a sign in. Record whether the company shows up at all, which page it shows up on, and who is sitting above it. Search Console, Analytics and paid keyword tools are out of bounds for this run, so never write a number that would need one. Anything you cannot see in public results reads Not Verified, with a line on what would settle it.

Pick exactly 3 competitors per prospect, and pick them only from companies that turned up in those same searches, sell the same main service and serve the same town or region. More than 3 qualify? Keep the ones appearing in the most of the 3 commercial searches, break a tie on the higher position in the results, and break whatever tie survives that on company name, A to Z. Fewer than 3 qualify and the comparison runs on the ones you found, with the shortfall named in the document and again in the closing message.

PageSpeed Insights at pagespeed.web.dev covers the home page and the single most important service page, for the prospect and for all 3 competitors. Record the mobile performance score, the desktop performance score, Largest Contentful Paint, Cumulative Layout Shift, Interaction to Next Paint wherever it is reported, and the 3 opportunities the report puts at the top. Whatever numbers the tool returns are the numbers. A score that will not come out is recorded as Not Verified with the exact error text, never as an estimate.

For social, take the public pages the prospect links from its own site, plus any public page that opens without an account. 3 readings each: the platform, the date of the newest public post, and how many posts went up across the 90 days ending 2026-09-01, which means 2026-06-03 onward. Same 3 readings for every competitor. Anything needing an account is out of bounds this run, Upwork, Freelancer, LinkedIn and Apollo included, and a social presence you cannot see without signing in reads Not Verified, not absent.

Score each prospect out of 100 across 5 components. Each component has its own scale, and the points it earned get written into the document and the closing message beside the reading that produced them.

The competitor gap carries 30. Read it off 9 checks, each of the 3 competitors against the prospect on 3 readings, where the prospect sits across the 3 commercial searches, the mobile performance score, and the count of public posts inside the 90 days ending 2026-09-01. 25 to 30 where 7 or more of those 9 checks go against the prospect, 18 to 24 where 5 or 6 do, 10 to 17 where 3 or 4 do, 1 to 9 where 1 or 2 do, and 0 where none do. Inside whichever range that lands in, go to the top of it where the widest single margin is wide, a competitor a full results page higher or 20 or more points of mobile performance ahead or 10 or more posts up against none, and to the bottom of it where every margin is slim. A reading that came back Not Verified counts as a check that did not go against the prospect.

Service fit carries 25, on how squarely the strongest gap sits inside 1 of the 4 services. 20 to 25 where the strongest gap fires exactly 1 of the 4 tests and every number behind that test is verified. 13 to 19 where it fires a test cleanly but 2 or more tests fired together and the ranking had to settle which one leads, or 1 number behind the winning test reads Not Verified. 6 to 12 where the gap sits nearest a service without firing its test outright, missing the threshold by 5 points of mobile performance or by half a second of Largest Contentful Paint or by 1 competitor short of the count the test asks for. 1 to 5 where the gap is real but sits outside all 4 tests and the nearest service is a stretch. 0 where no test fires and the primary service reads None Recommended.

Business fit carries 20, read off the Industry and Location fields against the customer profile in the Google Doc Agency Services and Ideal Customer. 16 to 20 where the Industry and the Location both sit inside the profile. 10 to 15 where 1 of the 2 sits inside it and the other sits next to it, a neighbouring industry or a town inside a region the profile names. 4 to 9 where 1 sits inside and the other sits outside. 1 to 3 where neither sits inside and nothing in the profile rules the prospect out. 0 where the profile rules it out. If that document will not open on either route, score this component against the 4 services named here, cap it at 10, and mark it Not Verified.

Verification carries 15, on how much of the case someone else could open again. Count the findings written into that prospect's document. 13 to 15 where every finding carries a source that reopens and nothing on the prospect reads Not Verified. 9 to 12 where 1 or 2 items read Not Verified and neither of them sits under the strongest gap. 5 to 8 where 3 or 4 items read Not Verified, or 1 does and it sits under the strongest gap. 1 to 4 where more than half the findings read Not Verified. 0 where the strongest gap itself is unverified.

Reachability carries 10, on how reachable the buying decision looks. 8 to 10 where the decision maker is verified on 2 independent public sources, the role is 1 of those attached to the recommended service, and the site publishes a contact route that works. 5 to 7 where the decision maker is verified but the role sits away from the recommended service, or the only route in is a general enquiry form. 2 to 4 where the decision maker reads Not Verified while the Contact Email on the Jira issue is filled and the site publishes a working way to get in touch. 0 or 1 where the decision maker reads Not Verified and the Contact Email on the Jira issue is the only route there is.

70 and above is High, 40 to 69 is Medium, anything under 40 is Low. 2 caps override the arithmetic: a prospect carrying fewer than 3 findings backed by a page or a tool output someone could open again scores 55 at most, and a prospect whose decision maker could not be verified scores 69 at most. A tie on the total goes to the larger competitor gap, and a tie surviving that goes to the lower issue number.

What the agency sells, and who it sells to, comes from the Google Doc Agency Services and Ideal Customer in the Google Drive folder Sales Prospect Research. If that document will not open on either route, fall back to the 4 services named here and flag it in the closing message. 1 primary service comes out of this, only 1, SEO or Paid Advertising or Social Media Marketing or Website Optimization. Website Optimization fires when the mobile performance score sits under 50, or Largest Contentful Paint runs past 4 seconds on mobile, or the site gives a visitor no working way to make contact. SEO fires when the company is missing from 2 pages of results on 2 of the 3 commercial searches while 2 or more of its competitors sit on page 1. Paid Advertising fires on that same absence, but only where the company's own site converts cleanly, meaning a mobile performance score of 50 or better and a contact route that works. Social Media Marketing fires when the newest public post predates 2026-06-03 and 2 or more competitors posted inside that window. Where more than 1 test fires, Website Optimization outranks SEO, SEO outranks Paid Advertising, Paid Advertising outranks Social Media Marketing. The tests that fired and lost get written down as secondary services, each with the evidence that fired it. If no test fires at all, the primary service reads None Recommended and the reason goes in the document.

Search the 1 signed in Gmail account next, across all mail, Sent, Archive and Spam included, for the company domain taken from the Company Website field and for the company name, over 2024-09-01 to 2026-09-01. What comes back changes what gets written. A thread whose newest message is dated 2026-06-03 or later is a live conversation somebody already owns: no draft gets written for that prospect at all, the tracker Outreach Status reads Active Thread Do Not Draft, and the Jira comment names who sent that newest message and when. A thread older than that is history the draft opens on, naming the last thing discussed and its date instead of introducing the agency cold. Nothing found reads No Previous Contact. Messages sitting in Drafts count for none of this, so a draft left behind by an earlier run of this same workflow never reads as a live conversation.

Name a decision maker only where 2 independent public sources agree on both the person and the role, so a team page on the company site and a public directory listing, or the company site and a signed article carrying a byline. Prefer a role attached to the recommended service: Founder, Chief Executive, Marketing Head, Marketing Director, Growth Manager or Business Owner. 1 source on its own reads Not Verified, and so does a name that only shows up behind a sign in. In that case the draft goes to the Contact Email on the Jira issue and opens to the company rather than to a person. Personal phone numbers and personal addresses go into none of the outputs, and the contact route is whatever the company publishes for enquiries.

1 Google Doc per prospect goes in the Google Drive folder Sales Prospect Research, in My Drive of the 1 signed in Google account. Name it Prospect_Analysis_ plus the company name with spaces swapped for underscores plus _2026-09-01, so Prospect_Analysis_ABC_Technologies_2026-09-01. Sharing stays exactly as the folder already has it and nothing gets shared outside the workspace. 15 things belong in every document: the company and what it sells, the site findings with the page each one came from, the PageSpeed numbers for the prospect and the 3 competitors, the search visibility reading per commercial search, the social reading, the 3 competitors with what each one does better, the strongest gap, the score with all 5 components written out, the band, the primary service with the test that fired it, the secondary services, the decision maker with the 2 sources or the words Not Verified, the Gmail history result, the sales angle in 80 words or fewer, and every item marked Not Verified with what would settle it. Lay the document out however reads best, that part is a judgment call.

The Google Sheet Sales Prospect Research Tracker sits in that same Sales Prospect Research folder, on the tab Prospects, and ends the run with 1 row per prospect, matched on column A and updated in place. Headers are on row 1, the rows start at row 2, and the columns run A Company Name, B Company Website, C Jira Issue Key, D Research Status, E Main Gap, F Recommended Service, G Secondary Services, H Competitor Gap, I Priority Score, J Priority Band, K Decision Maker, L Decision Maker Status, M Gmail History, N Report Link, O Outreach Status, P Next Action, Q Last Updated. Dates go in as YYYY-MM-DD and column Q reads 2026-09-01. A filled row looks like this: ABC Technologies, abctechnologies.com, SP-4, Research Complete, Missing from all 3 commercial searches, SEO, Website Optimization, 3 competitors on page 1, 66, Medium, Not Verified, 1 source only, No Previous Contact, the document link, Draft Ready Not Sent, Send the draft by 2026-09-04, 2026-09-01. Column P reads the action, then the word by, then a date, and that date comes off 2026-09-01 and the band the prospect landed in. High is 2 days on, so 2026-09-03. Medium is 3 days on, so 2026-09-04. Low is 5 days on, which falls on a Sunday this run and so rolls to 2026-09-07, because a date landing on a Saturday or a Sunday moves to the Monday after it. The action in front of that date reads Send the draft where a draft is sitting in Drafts, Reply on the existing thread where the live thread rule blocked the draft, and Save the draft by hand and send it where Gmail refused to save one. The Jira comment carries that same wording and that same date as its next action. Research Status reads Research Complete. Outreach Status reads Draft Ready Not Sent, or Active Thread Do Not Draft where the Gmail rule blocked the draft, or Draft Blocked where Gmail refused to save one. A company with no row on Prospects gets one appended at the bottom of that tab with those same columns filled. Nothing else on the sheet changes and no row gets removed.

1 Gmail draft per prospect sits unsent in the Drafts folder of the 1 signed in account, addressed to the Contact Email on that prospect's Jira issue, subject reading Prospect Outreach followed by the company name, so Prospect Outreach ABC Technologies. The body runs 120 to 180 words and carries 2 findings with the evidence behind each, 1 thing a named competitor does better, the single primary service and what it would fix, and 1 question the reader can answer in a line. No attachments. No links to anything outside the prospect's own site. No promise about rankings, traffic, leads or revenue. Nothing gets sent, scheduled or queued from this run, not through a connector and not through a browser, and the draft stays in Drafts for the BDE to send by hand. If Gmail refuses to save a draft, the full email text goes into that prospect's Google Doc under the label Draft Email Not Saved with the exact error text beside it, and no attempt gets made to send that mail some other way.

Both Jira issues end the run carrying a comment and a status. The comment holds the priority score with its band, the primary service, the secondary services, the strongest gap and the evidence behind it, the competitor that beat the prospect there, the Google Doc name and link, the decision maker with the verification status, the Gmail history result, and the next action with the date it should happen by. Status moves from Pending to Research Complete. No new project, no new board, no new status, no new issue type, and nothing moves to a status meaning contacted, because nothing was sent.

Run this 2 times on 2026-09-01 and nothing should get written 2 times. Before anything gets written, search the Sales Prospect Research folder for the same filename, the Prospects tab for the company name, Gmail Drafts for the same subject, and the Jira issue for a comment already carrying 2026-09-01, then update what is already there in place instead of making a 2nd copy. 1 document, 1 row, 1 draft and 1 comment per company, however many times this runs.

All of this assumes things that are already sitting there: a signed in Jira Cloud site carrying the project Sales Prospecting, key SP, the board Prospect Pipeline and the statuses Pending and Research Complete, a Google Drive folder Sales Prospect Research in My Drive holding the Google Doc Agency Services and Ideal Customer and the Google Sheet Sales Prospect Research Tracker with its Prospects tab, and 1 signed in Gmail account. Reach each app through its connected integration where that works. Where the integration is missing, errors, times out, refuses the call or hands back nothing while the screen is plainly showing something, go through the Chrome browser plugin instead. The accounts are already signed in in Chrome, so that route is open for every app named here. Nothing counts as missing, unavailable, unauthorised or blocked until the Chrome browser plugin has been pointed at it and failed too. A connector that was never set up, a message saying there is no tool for that job, a permission refusal, a page that will not load, each one is a reason to open the app in Chrome and carry on, never a reason to stop and never a reason to report an app as unavailable. A step reported as blocked without the Chrome browser plugin having been tried on it is a failed run, whatever else got finished. Only 2 things stop anything: Jira failing on both routes, which stops the lot, and a prospect's own website failing on both routes, which stops that prospect and nothing else. Report anything blocked with the exact error text each route returned. Everything else gets recorded and worked around, a PageSpeed run that will not complete, a competitor site that will not open, a Drive document or a sheet row or a draft that will not save. Name it, say what it cost the analysis, mark whatever it left unproven as Not Verified, and finish every part of the run that does not depend on it. Never invent a page, a score, a search position, a competitor, a person, a document, a sheet row, a draft or an error message, and never report something as opened, created or saved where it was not.

The run is done when both selected Jira issues read Research Complete and carry the comment described above, 2 Google Docs sit in Sales Prospect Research under the exact filename format, 2 rows on the Prospects tab hold every column named above, and 1 unsent Gmail draft per prospect sits in Drafts unless the live thread rule or a save failure blocked it. The closing message then names the 2 issues taken with what ruled the others out, each score with its 5 components, each band, each primary service with the test that fired it, every item marked Not Verified, every prospect where no draft was written and why, every step that fell back to the Chrome browser plugin with the error that sent it there, and every step still blocked with the error from both routes.


2. Workflow type (examples: prospecting, qualification, onboarding, account management, or renewal preparation) *

Prospecting - working queued prospects off a Jira backlog into an evidence-backed research document, a scored priority band with a single recommended service, a tracker row, and a prepared (unsent) outreach draft.

3. Is this a pre-sales or post-sales workflow? *

Pre-sales

4. Typical frequency of this task *

Weekly - each run takes the two oldest qualifying SP issues off the Prospect Pipeline board and works them end to end, with the next action dated off the run date and the band (High two days out, Medium three, Low five, rolling to the Monday after if it lands on a weekend, so a Medium prospect on 2026-09-01 is due 2026-09-04). The run is idempotent by design - filename, tracker row, draft subject and Jira comment are all matched before anything is written - so a repeat run on the same date updates in place rather than duplicating.
